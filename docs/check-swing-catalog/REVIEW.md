# Check-swing footage review — 2026-10-29

Six primary subagents screened 338 discovery leads and visually inspected 100 source-video candidates (some overlap across batches). Independent reviewers audited proposed additions. Forty-four new swings passed, bringing the reviewed game catalog from 6 to 50.

The expanded catalog contains 33 swing rulings and 17 no-swing rulings, with 32 first-base-side and 18 third-base-side replays. Ejection-based sourcing is still biased toward disputed calls.

New clips are available in practice immediately on publication and enter daily selection on 2026-10-30 UTC. The original daily questions, stored answers, and share codes for earlier dates remain unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- Publicly accessible MLB footage and a documented on-field ruling.
- Actual first- or third-base-side replay with the complete meaningful check visible.
- Exact start/end window excluding decision signals and revealing captions.
- Primary visual review plus independent acceptance; no inference from the reviewer’s opinion of the swing.
- Official game data resolves conflicting descriptions and game/publication dates.

## Findings that changed decisions

- Two proposed windows showed a reset or missed meaningful swing onset; both were removed.
- Profar’s clean standalone window was held because the usable span was only about 1.1 seconds.
- Myers and several ejection clips show strike two, not the later strikeout pitch.
- Realmuto’s report contradicted itself; the official game record confirms the no-swing walk.
- Turner’s MLB description and official game feed disagree about review procedure; the catalog uses the official final swing ruling and avoids naming an unverified individual umpire.
- Very short accepted windows (Wood 1.5 seconds, Díaz 1.6 seconds) contain the full check; the client respects the round timing gate before saving.
