# Sixth expansion batch: 2026-10-31

Adds 30 independently audited pitches, taking the library from 150 to 180. Practice access is immediate; daily eligibility starts November 1 UTC. The previously published 150 entries retain their exact values and order, preserving October 31’s challenge and earlier results.

This batch uses MLB HBP-review footage and event-first retrieval of alternate highlights and condensed games. Primary footage review and a separate reviewer verified complete checking motion, usable side view, exact pitch identity/date, the recorded result, caller attribution where established, and duplicate identity. Seven marginal avoidance/onset cases were rejected during these audit groups rather than added to meet a numeric target.

A final HBP or ball ruling can establish no swing even when the source does not document a separate appeal. Those contexts disclose the distinction. Unknown swing decision-makers are explicitly unidentified; crew assignments and ejection officials are not used to invent appeal attribution. Where evidence identifies only the plate umpire’s final award, the context explains that scope.

Audit corrections remove result-revealing endings, clear dissolves, repair count descriptions, and distinguish HBP/ball awards from explicit appeals. The public provenance JSON retains each accepted pitch’s reviewed source and independent audit. Video files continue to use the existing publisher-hosted streams; no additional local media assets are introduced.

The target remains 500, with 320 more verified clips needed. Caption, audio-transcript and historical event indexes remain discovery queues; none of their unreviewed counts are included in the playable total.

## Daily rotation from November 1

The larger pool exposed low-bit bias in the original seeded shuffle: a 5,000-day simulation with 180 clips selected the least-used clip 15 times and the most-used 515 times, against a mean of about 139. Starting November 1, deterministic daily draws use a better-mixed 32-bit generator. The legacy generator remains in use for every earlier date, so played challenges and scorecards do not change. Client and API share the same date gate and algorithm.
