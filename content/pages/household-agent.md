Household Agent is Paul Glenn's private personal assistant, powered by OpenClaw. It helps an authorized user search, read, and summarize their own Gmail messages through a private, authenticated interface. It is not a public email service and has no public signup.

## Gmail access

The current integration requests read-only Gmail permission and basic Google account identity. It can search and read messages, but cannot send mail, delete messages, change labels or read status, or modify mailbox settings. Calendar and Drive access are not requested.

When Gmail information is used to answer a request, relevant email content and metadata are processed by the assistant and may be sent to OpenAI for the requested AI response. Connecting Gmail does not publish the mailbox on this website.
